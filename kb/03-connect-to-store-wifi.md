# Connect to the store Wi-Fi

| | |
|---|---|
| **Knowledge base** | IT Self-Service |
| **Category** | Network & Wi-Fi |
| **Audience** | Store employees and visiting head office staff |
| **Version** | 2.0 – network name changed to NPK-Store |

## Which network?

| Network | Who | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **NPK-Store** | Company laptops and handheld scanners | All work systems |
| **NPK-Guest** | Personal phones, vendors | Internet only – no company systems |

Registers and back-office PCs are wired and don't need Wi-Fi.

## Company laptop
Company laptops connect to **NPK-Store automatically**. If yours doesn't:
1. Click the **Wi-Fi icon** at the bottom right of the screen.
2. Select **NPK-Store** → **Connect**.
3. If asked, sign in with your **company username and password**.
4. Tick **Connect automatically**.

## Personal phone
1. Open **Wi-Fi settings** and choose **NPK-Guest**.
2. Open your browser. The welcome page appears — accept the terms.
3. Guest access lasts **12 hours**, then you accept the terms again.

## Not connecting?
- **Turn Wi-Fi off and on**, or restart the laptop.
- **Changed your password recently?** Forget the network (right-click → *Forget*) and connect again with your new password.
- **Only you, or everyone?** If nobody in the store can connect, call the Service Desk straight away and tell us your **store number** — we treat that as urgent.
