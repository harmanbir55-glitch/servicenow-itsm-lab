# Set up VPN on a company laptop

| | |
|---|---|
| **Knowledge base** | IT Self-Service |
| **Category** | Remote Access |
| **Audience** | Head office staff and managers who work remotely |

## What is VPN and when do I need it?
VPN securely connects your laptop to the NorthPeak network when you're away from the office or a store. You need it for **shared drives** and **internal systems**. You **don't** need it for Outlook, Teams or OneDrive.

## Connect
1. Connect to the internet (home Wi-Fi or a hotspot).
2. Click the **up arrow** on the taskbar → **NorthPeak VPN** icon.
3. Click **Connect**.
4. Sign in with your **company username and password**.
5. Approve the prompt on your **Authenticator app**.
6. The icon turns **green** when connected.

The VPN app is already installed on company laptops. If you can't find it, open **Company Portal** → search **NorthPeak VPN** → **Install**.

## Disconnect
Click the VPN icon → **Disconnect**. Disconnect when you're finished so video calls stay fast.

## Common problems

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| "Authentication failed" | Check Caps Lock. Changed your password recently? Sign in to a company PC once first. |
| No Authenticator prompt | Open the Authenticator app manually and pull down to refresh. |
| Connects, then drops | Move closer to your router or switch networks. Hotel Wi-Fi often blocks VPN — try your phone's hotspot. |
| Connected but shared drives missing | Disconnect and reconnect, then restart File Explorer. |

## Still stuck?
Contact the Service Desk with the **error message** (a photo of the screen is fine) and the **time** it happened.
