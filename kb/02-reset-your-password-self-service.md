# Reset your password with self-service

| | |
|---|---|
| **Knowledge base** | IT Self-Service |
| **Category** | Accounts & Passwords |
| **Audience** | All NorthPeak employees |

## Forgot your password or locked out?
You can reset it yourself in about 2 minutes, any time of day, without calling the Service Desk.

## One-time setup (do this now, while you can still sign in)
1. Sign in to the **Service Portal**.
2. Click your name (top right) → **Password Reset Enrollment**.
3. Add your **mobile phone number** and/or answer the **security questions**.
4. Click **Save**.

If you skip this step, you'll need to call the Service Desk when you forget your password.

## Reset your password
1. Go to the portal login page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your **username** (for example `jane.doe`) and complete the check.
3. Choose how to verify: a **code sent to your phone**, or your **security questions**.
4. Enter a new password:
   - at least **12 characters**
   - a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols
   - not one of your recent passwords
   💡 A short sentence works well: `Paint-aisle-opens-at-7!`
5. Click **Reset Password**. You can sign in straight away.

## After you reset
Update your password on your **phone** (Outlook, Teams and Wi-Fi). Old passwords saved on devices are the most common cause of repeat lockouts.

## Still stuck?
Call the IT Service Desk. We'll verify your identity and help you reset it. We'll **never** ask for your current password.
