## What you might see
- New emails aren't arriving, but colleagues say they sent them.
- The bottom of Outlook says **Disconnected**, **Working offline** or **Trying to connect**.
- Sent emails are stuck in the **Outbox**.

## Try these in order

1. **Check you're online.** Open a website. If nothing loads, it's your connection, not Outlook.
2. **Turn off Work Offline.** In Outlook, go to **Send / Receive** → make sure **Work Offline** is *not* highlighted.
3. **Restart Outlook.** Close it completely, wait 10 seconds, open it again.
4. **Check Outlook on the web.** Go to outlook.office.com and sign in.
   - Emails are there → the problem is Outlook on your computer; continue to step 5.
   - Emails aren't there either → call the Service Desk.
5. **Restart your computer.** This fixes most sync problems.
6. **Large attachment stuck in the Outbox?** Open the Outbox, delete or drag the email to Drafts, and send the file as a OneDrive link instead.

## Still not syncing?
Contact the Service Desk and tell us what the bottom bar of Outlook says and whether Outlook on the web works. That saves several steps.
