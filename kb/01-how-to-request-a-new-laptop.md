# How to request and set up a new laptop

| | |
|---|---|
| **Knowledge base** | IT Self-Service |
| **Category** | Hardware |
| **Audience** | All NorthPeak employees |
| **Related catalog item** | New Laptop Request |

## Before you start
You can request a laptop if yours is broken, too slow for your role, out of warranty, or you're new and don't have one yet. Managers can request one on behalf of a team member.

## Which laptop should I pick?

| Model | Best for | Approval needed |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** – Dell Latitude | Email, Office, Teams, store and office systems | Your manager |
| **Developer** – Lenovo ThinkPad P16s | Software development, design, data work | Your manager **and** IT Manager |
| **Executive** – MacBook Pro 14 | Directors and above | Your manager **and** IT Manager |

Adding accessories can bring a Standard request over $1,500, which also needs IT Manager approval.

## Request it
1. Open the **Service Portal** and go to **Hardware → New Laptop Request**.
2. Check **Requested for** (it's you by default — change it if you're requesting for someone else).
3. Pick the **model** and any **accessories**. The price updates as you go.
4. Explain **why** you need it in *Business justification* (for example "Current laptop is 5 years old and won't run Teams video").
5. Pick a **Needed by** date. Please allow **5 business days**.
6. If it replaces an old laptop, choose **Yes** and enter the asset tag from the sticker on the bottom (looks like `NP-LT-00001`).
7. Click **Order Now**.

## What happens next
You'll get an email at each step:
1. **Request received**
2. **Approval** by your manager (and IT Manager, if needed)
3. IT images and sets up your laptop
4. **Ready for pickup** — collect it from the IT Service Desk at your location

You can check progress any time under **My Requests** in the portal.

## When you pick it up
- Bring your **old laptop** and charger if you're replacing one.
- Sign in with your usual company username and password.
- Your files in OneDrive sync automatically. Give it a few minutes on first sign-in.

## Need help?
Contact the IT Service Desk through the portal or by phone.
